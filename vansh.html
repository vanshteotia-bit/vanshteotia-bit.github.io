02 — How I Think

I like to understand why people interact with brands, what makes a product meaningful and how stories influence decisions.

I look at how data helps businesses grow, and I enjoy seeing a product from both the user’s and the business’s perspective: spotting gaps, interpreting information and turning observations into actionable improvements.

Content, positioning, outreach and analysis, learned by doing.

As a founding member and content strategist at Escaleit, I worked across 7+ client projects covering content planning, digital marketing, positioning and promotional initiatives.

With IEEE RIT-B I gained experience in public relations, event promotion, sponsorship outreach and communication, engaging 5+ prospective sponsors and partners.

Software testing and data analytics internships taught me to examine products closely and read data carefully. I use AI tools such as Claude and Claude Code to speed up research, experimentation and creative workflows.
